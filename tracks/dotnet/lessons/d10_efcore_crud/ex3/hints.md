## Hint 1

Validate, build, `Add`, `SaveChangesAsync`, answer. The `Location` header needs
the new id — and the new id does not exist until one of those steps has
happened.

Work out which, and everything after it falls into place.

## Hint 2

The id is generated by the database, so it is only populated **after**
`await db.SaveChangesAsync()`. Read `member.Id` then:

```csharp
return TypedResults.Created($"/d10/members/{member.Id}", member);
```

Three validations — blank name, blank email, negative credits — and all of them
before `db.Members.Add(...)`. The gate counts rows after three rejected
requests, because a 400 returned *after* an `Add` leaves that entity staged in
the change tracker.

New members start at grade `"white"`; that is your code's decision, not the
client's.
