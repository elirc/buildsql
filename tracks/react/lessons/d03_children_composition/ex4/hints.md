## Hint 1
A slot is just a prop whose value is markup. This component has three of them -
two named, one called `children` - and its whole job is deciding what element
each one lands in.

## Hint 2
Landmarks are elements, not attributes: `<header>`, `<aside>` and `<main>` come
with the banner, complementary and main roles already attached, as long as they
are not nested inside another sectioning element that would change what they
mean.
