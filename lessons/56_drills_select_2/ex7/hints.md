## Hint 1
`*` binds tighter than `+`, exactly as in arithmetic. The bracketed
version therefore doubles the price before it takes five percent.

## Hint 2
Do not simplify either expression. `(price + price) * 0.05` is the same
as `price * 0.1`, but writing it that way loses the point of the exercise -
and the two forms do not always land on the same `numeric` scale.
