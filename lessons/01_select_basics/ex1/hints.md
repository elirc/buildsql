## Hint 1
You need two of the six columns of `products`, in a specific order. No
filtering, no dedup — every row comes back.

## Hint 2
`SELECT <first column>, <second column> FROM products;` — the SELECT list is
comma-separated and its order is the output order.

## Hint 3
The two columns are `name` and `price`, in that order, and the table is
`products`. Assemble the one-line query yourself.
